## Types of cells
1. prokaryotic cell (原核细胞): 
	1. e.g. bacteria, archaea
	2. most have cell wall
	3. have ribosomes
	4. some bacteria have flagella
	5. extra capsule 荚膜 outside cell wall for protection
	6. ==do not have any membrane-bound organelles==
2. eukaryotic cell (真核细胞):
	1. more complex, larger

## Vocab
1. cytoplasm 细胞质
2. nucleoid 类核
3. plasma membrane 细胞膜
4. flagella 鞭毛: long projections used for mobility

## Plasma membrane
1. called a **phospholipid bilayer**
2. *hydrophobic* fatty acid tails (nonpolar) face inward, *hydrophilic* phosphate heads (polar) face outward
3. semipermeable: only hydrophobic molecules (e.g. O_2 CO_2) pass through unaided
4. types of plasma membrane proteins (carbohydrate side chains attached to the surface of some proteins)
	1. adhesion proteins
	2. receptor proteins (e.g. hormones)
	3. transport proteins: use ATP to transport solutes across the membrane
	4. cell surface markers (e.g. glycoproteins, glycolipids): cell recognition
5. arrangement of phospholipids and proteins is known as the **fluid-mosaic model**

ribosomes 核糖体
site of protein synthesis

rough ER: intracellular transport, produce proteins to build Golgi bodies, lysosomes, or the ER
smooth ER: produce lipids, hormone, and steroid, break down toxins

Golgi Body modifies, processes, sorts, and distributes proteins, produce lysosomes

线粒体 mitochondria transforms mechanical energy stored in carbohydrates and lipids to ATP

溶酶体 Lysosomes carry digestive enzymes, breaks down waste, debris, large ingested particles

中心体 Centrioles are active during cell division, not present in plant cells

液泡 Vacuoles are only present in plants, fluid-filled sacs that store water, waste, food

过氧物酶体 Peroxisomes detoxify substances with byproduct H2O2, present in liver and kidney cells

Cytoskeleton 2 types are microtubules and microfilaments, both important for movement; 

cilia 纤毛
flagella 鞭毛
