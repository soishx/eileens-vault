## Critical Thinking
7 types of question based on these skills: 
- Identifying the Main Conclusion, 
- Drawing a Conclusion, 
- Identifying an Assumption, 
- **Assessing the Impact of Additional Evidence,** 
- Detecting Reasoning Errors, 
- **Matching Arguments,** 
- and Applying Principles.

### Matching Arguments
- identify similarity in the **structure** or the **pattern** of arguments 
- sometimes the argument is invalid
