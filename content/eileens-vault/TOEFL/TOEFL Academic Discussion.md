## 信息类
主要问通过什么获取信息最好（家人朋友，社交媒体，传统新闻）
1. 网络类（社交媒体相关）可以用科技类思路
	1. increase efficiency
		1. Online platforms **streamline** the search for information, allowing users to quickly find the most relevant and resources and reviews with just a few clicks.
		2. Quick access to real-time updates and user-generated content **speeds up the decision-making process**, eliminating the need to visit multiple sources.
	2. provide diverse options/perspectives, diversity, more choices
		1. Social media platforms expose users to **a broad spectrum of perspectives**, ensuring that they can explore multiple viewpoints before making decisions.
		2. Online reviews and forums **bring together voices from diverse backgrounds**, offering insights that may not be available through traditional sources.
	3. spread of misinformation and fake news
		1. Social media is highly susceptible to the spread of misinformation and fake news, as **content often circulates without proper verification**.
	4. lack of depth and context
		1. Information on social media is often presented in bite-sized, simplified formats, which can lack the depth and context needed to fully understand complex issues.
		2. Social media posts and articles may provide superficial overviews without exploring **underlying causes or nuanced perspectives**, leading to a limited understanding of the topic.
2. 通过家人朋友名人获取信息
	1. personalization and relevance
		1. friends and family usually know **our needs, preferences, and interests**, so the information they provide is often tailored and relevant to us.
		2. They are likely to consider our circumstances when giving advice, making the information they share **more practical and useful for our specific situation**.
	2. accessibility and convenience
		1. Friends and family are usually **easy to reach for quick questions**, so getting information from them can be faster than searching online or consulting professionals.
	3. bias and subjectivity
		1. Friends and family may unintentionally provide biased information based on their personal beliefs, experiences, or preferences, which may not be universally applicable.
		2. Their advice may reflect their own biases or preconceived notions, limiting the objectivity and balance of the information they offer.
	4. limited knowledge and expertise
		1. may lack expertise in certain areas, so the information they provide might be incomplete or inaccurate.
		2. Depending too heavily on friends and family can mean **missing out on professional insights or broader perspectives from experts.**

## 题型
1. agree/disagree
2. what is the best way/solution
3. which one is better

结构：
1. 第一段: Thesis statement: I believe. . . , as it. . . (and. . . ) (最好写两个理由)
2. 第二段: 理由一 topic sentence+论述展开（3句话）+具体例子（2-3句）
3. 第三段: 理由二 topic sentence+论述展开/让步（3句话）
	- which one is better 一定要让步，可以不写理由二
	- agree/disagree, what is the best way/solution，可以写理由二，也可以让步（让步可以用NPC观点，也可自己编）

让步：
- Some people might argue that. . . . However, I believe that/this perspective overlooks. . . .
- While some might argue that. . . , I contend that [drawbacks] outweigh these potential benefits.
