### Long Run Costs
- choose between fixed cost levels: High fixed costs will get you more money if your sales are high, but they waste money if your sales are low
![[Pasted image 20241110120330.png|400]]

higher fixed cost decreases variable cost at any given ouput level
- The long-run average total cost curve shows the relationship between output and average total cost ==when fixed cost has been chosen to minimize average total cost for each level of output.==
![[Pasted image 20241110121405.png|300]]
- economies of scale: LRATC falls as output rises
- diseconomies of scale: LRATC rises as output rises
- Increasing returns to scale - Output is increasing at a higher rate than inputs
- Decreasing returns to scale - Output is increasing at a lower rate than inputs
- Constant returns to scale - Output is increasing at a constant rate to input.

## Marginal Analysis
- Law of diminishing marginal utility
- Marginal analysis: how to maximize utility:
	1. calculate marginal utility per dollar for each quantity
	2. compare this number, choose from the highest to lowest until you run out of money
