## Types
1. Regressive – Poor people pay a higher percentage of income than rich people.
2. Progressive tax – The rich pay a higher percentage of income than poor people.  
3. Flat (proportional) tax – Rich and poor pay an equal percentage.

> [!Definition] Excise tax
> a tax on sales of a particular good or service

A tax of $40 is levied
- consumers pay $20 more than they would have
- producers make $20 less than they would have
- the equilibrium quantity is less (inefficiency)
- [[Taxes#^95a158|how the tax is distributed depends on elasticities of supply and demand]]

> [!NOTE]
> the effect is the same no matter the tax is levied on consumers or producers, but the moving curve differs
> 

- If PED is low and the PES is high, the tax falls mainly on consumers  ^95a158
- If PED is high and PES is low, the tax falls mainly on producers
- ==more inelastic (vertical), more tax==

PED=price elasticity of demand

PES=price elasticity of supply
