## Marginal Analysis
- Marginal benefit slopes down, Marginal cost slopes up
- ==benefit is maximized at MB=MC==
- Diminishing marginal utility (MU)
- ==Utility maximizing combinations: MU_x/P_x = MU_y/P_y==
## Price Elasticity of Demand
![[Pasted image 20241112071253.png|300]]
P up, TR down: PED>1 (relatively elastic)
P up, TR up: PED<1 (relatively inelastic)

more vertical curve of demand and supply (less elastic): more tax

**Production function**: relation between total product (TP) and quantity
**Marginal Product (MP)** = ΔTP/ΔQ of labor
**Law of diminishing marginal returns**: increasing returns→decreasing returns→negative returns
**MP is used to calculate MC**: MC = wage/MP

Long run: all costs are variable; can change the maximum production level of the  business
Short run: can change the rate of production

**Long run ATC**: increasing returns to scale (double all inputs, more than double output)→constant returns to scale (double all inputs, double output)→decreasing returns to scale (double all inputs, less than double output)

## Perfectly competitive markets 
- are always allocatively efficient because P=MC 
- are productively efficient in the long run bc they produce at min. ATC
- If AVC<P<ATC, a firm's loss is less than it would have lost if it shut down bc it has to pay FC that is greater than the loss

## Monopolies
- are not allocatively efficient (socially optimal) bc P>MC
- are not productively efficient bc they don't typically minimize their costs to min. ATC

## Monopolistic Competition
- **Definition**
	1. many competing producers with **differentiated products**
	2. **free entry and exit in the long run**

![[Pasted image 20241112112620.png|300]]
- **At equilibrium**
	- P=ATC
	- Q: MR=MC
	- productively inefficient and allocatively inefficient
	- normal profit (0 economic profit)
- **Profit**
	- P> ATC
	- **Firms will enter**, so each individual firm has a smaller share of the market, so the **D and MR both shift to the left** until there is zero economic profit (P=ATC)
	- back to long run
- **Loss**
	- P< ATC
	- **Firms will exit, so D and MR both shift to the right**
