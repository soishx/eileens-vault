## Consumer & Producer Surplus
- Consumer surplus is the price one is willing to pay minus the price paid (or market price)
- Producer surplus is the money the producer sells for minus the money they were willing to sell for.  
- ![[Pasted image 20241110110147.png|400]]

Markets
- Markets maximize total surplus.
- The people who “want” the product most will get it.
- The producers most willing to sell will get the sale.
- When both sides benefit, the sale happens
- When both sides don’t, the sale does not.  





> [!info] Efficiency
> Effciency is achieved when total surplus is maximized


> [!important]
> Once the market is in equilibrium, there is no way to increase the gains from trade. Any other outcomes reduces total surplus

1. reallocate consumption among consumers
take a unit away from a consumer who values it more and give it to someone who values it less (reduces consumer surplus)
2. reallocate sales among sellers
force a producer who would sell a good to keep it and force someone who would keep it to sell it (reduces producer surplus)
3. change the quantity traded

Equity (fairness) is often in conflict with efficiency
