- Implicit cost: a type of opportunity cost, dollar value of forgone benefit
- Explicit cost: money actually paid
- usually opportunity cost is break into implicit cost and explicit cost

- Economic profit = revenue - (implicit cost + explicit cost)

- **Normal profit**
	- **0 economic profit**
	- **equilibrium in perfect competition and monopolistic competition**
