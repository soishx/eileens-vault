• Contrasting words:
• However, though, that said尽管, that being said尽管, nevertheless尽管, nonetheless尽管, notwithstanding尽管, still尽管, yet尽管, granted尽管, Conversely相反的, alternatively引出替代方案, 取代关系 of course轻微转折, rather 而是，regardless 无论
