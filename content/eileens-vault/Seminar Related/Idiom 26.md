1. Add up: (说得通)
When figures/facts ___ __ , they make sense. It is often used negatively. 
(e.g. It doesn't add up.)
2. Ad hoc: (临时安排的)
Improvised, or made up on the spot. 
(e.g. we are going to get an ad hoc commitee right now to try to improve the instruction.)
3. After a fashion: (还过得去，还算可以)
If you can do something ___ , you can do it, but not well. 
(- Can Joe play the violin? - Well...... After a fashion.)
4. After hours: (工作时间之后，下班后)
After normal hours, or after closing time.
(e.g. I go to that restaurant after hours because my uncle owns it, and he lets me in and feeds me when the door is closed.)
5. After the fact: (事后)
After something actually occurs.
(- It seems like you didn't prepare for the test. - Well I didn't, but you're saying that after the fact.)
6. Against all odds: (尽管困难重重；在极为不利的情况下)
Used to describe something that happens but very unlikely. (e.g. Against all odds, he recovered.)
