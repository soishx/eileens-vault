## Experiment & Observation
**stratified random sample's advantage:**
1. reduces sampling variability
2. proportionally selects individuals from each group, more representative
**simple random sample's disadvantage**:
may result in a sample that contains too many individuals from one group

**cluster random sample 优缺点**
1. convenient
2. increases variability
3. not representative

**systematic random sample 缺点**
1. not convenient
2. sampling variability does not reduce

**replication 作用**
- replication: 每个 treatment 中的 experimental units 多于一个
- 作用: reduce the effect of natural variability due to differences between experimental units
- 如果没有：can't distinguish whether the effect is due to treatment or just due to chance variation (natural difference)

**randomization**
- reduce the effects of confounding variables on the explanatory variable and response variable
- 如果没有会造成 the result is not precise
